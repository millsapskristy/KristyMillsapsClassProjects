Common Behaviors

  • Purring — can mean comfort, but sometimes stress too.
  • Kneading — a comfort behavior from kittenhood.
  • Slow blinking — often a sign of trust.
  • Scratching — helps mark territory and maintain claws.

Other Behaviors

Common cat behaviors often reflect their natural instincts as hunters and independent animals. Cats frequently groom themselves to stay clean and regulate their scent, and they may knead soft surfaces as a comforting habit learned from kittenhood. Scratching is another normal behavior that helps them stretch, mark territory, and keep their claws healthy. Many cats also show affection by purring, rubbing against people, or following their owners around, while others prefer quiet observation from a distance. Playful actions like chasing toys, pouncing, or sudden bursts of energy, often called “zoomies,” are ways cats release energy and practice hunting skills. Understanding these behaviors helps owners better meet their cat’s physical and emotional needs.
